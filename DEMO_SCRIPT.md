## Recording Tips

1. **Quality**: Use 1080p minimum resolution
2. **Audio**: Clear narration, no background noise
3. **Pace**: Speak clearly and not too fast
4. **Transitions**: Smooth transitions between scenes
5. **Demo**: Show the full user flow without errors
6. **Branding**: Consistent colors and fonts

## Tools Recommended

- **Screen Recording**: OBS Studio, QuickTime (Mac), or Loom
- **Video Editing**: iMovie, DaVinci Resolve, or Adobe Premiere
- **Narration**: Audacity for audio recording
- **Graphics**: Canva or Figma for title screens
